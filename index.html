Manifest Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2023 • Developed by Zincstate • Status: Active

Clause 01

1. Introduction

Welcome to Manifest, a personal finance management application developed by Zincstate ("we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy.

This Privacy Policy applies to all information collected through our mobile application (Manifest) and any related services. Manifest is designed from the ground up as a local-first application, prioritizing your privacy by keeping your data on your device.

Clause 02

2. Information We Collect

Manifest is engineered as a local-first application. We prioritize your privacy by keeping your data strictly on your device:

Financial Data

We collect transaction history, account balances, and budget details that you manually enter or import via SMS. This data is stored locally on your device.

SMS Data (Local Parsing Only)

If you grant SMS permissions, the app parses financial SMS messages locally to automate expense tracking. We do NOT upload your SMS content to our servers. All text parsing algorithms run in-memory on your handset.

Cloud Backups (Google Drive)

If you enable Google Drive backups, we use your Google account to store an encrypted backup of your database in your own Google Drive. We do not have access to this data outside of the app's backup/restore functionality.

Clause 03

3. Purpose of Processing

We use your data locally on your device solely to:

  • Provide personal finance tracking and analysis.
  • Automate transaction entry from banking SMS notifications.
  • Provide user-directed data backup and restoration services.
  • Improve app performance and user experience locally on device.
Clause 04

4. Global Compliance

GDPR (European Union)

If you are from the European Economic Area, our legal basis for collecting and using personal information depends on the Personal Information we collect and the specific context in which we collect it. You retain the right to access, update, export, or delete your data at any time via device storage controls.

India (IT Act & SPDI Rules)

We comply with the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011. Data remains in user-owned sandboxes, eliminating remote interception risk.

US (CCPA / CPRA)

We do not sell your personal information. We have never monetized user telemetry or personal records. We respect your statutory rights to inspect and delete data.

Clause 05

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data. Since most data is stored locally, its security also depends on your device's security (e.g., strong screen lock, PIN, biometric lock).

Notice: Zincstate does not hold master keys or remote copies of your SQLite database.
