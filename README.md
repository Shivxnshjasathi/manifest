# Manifest — Financial Clarity, Automated.

![Manifest Header](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/zincstate/manifest/main/art/banner.png)

> **"Manifest your wealth by mastering your flow."**

Manifest is a modern, privacy-centric, and automated money manager built for individuals who demand financial clarity without the manual labor. It bridges the gap between complex accounting software and overly simple tracking apps, delivering a premium Material 3 experience that lives entirely on your device.

**Manifest changes the game.** We don’t just track transactions; we automate your financial awareness. Whether you’re splitting a dinner bill, tracking a business expense with a photo receipt, or importing bank alerts from SMS, Manifest handles the heavy lifting so you can focus on growing your net worth.

## ✨ Feature Showcase

### 🧠 **Smart Automation & NLP**
Stop typing. Descriptions like *"spent 500 on dinner from SBI"* are automatically parsed into Amount, Category, and Account. 

### 📩 **SMS One-Tap Import**
Manifest scans your bank transaction alerts and lets you import them with a single tap. No more manual entry of every cup of coffee.

### 👥 **Advanced Expense Splitting**
The only app that understands "Net Share." When you split an expense, Manifest tracks the full transaction for your bank balance but only counts **your share** toward your monthly budget and totals.

### 📸 **Receipt Cloud-Free Storage**
Snap a photo of your bills. They are stored securely in your app's internal storage—linked to the transaction, indexed for search, and never shared with a third party.

### 🗓️ **Multifaceted Financial Views**
*   **Daily:** A chronological stream of your life.
*   **Calendar:** Dual-color badges (Income/Expense) for every day.
*   **Weekly:** ISO-standard week grouping to see mid-month trends.
*   **Monthly:** Category-level breakdown with interactive donut charts.
*   **Summary:** High-level totals and Net Worth tracking.

### 🔒 **Bank-Grade Local Security**
Biometric Lock (Fingerprint/Face) ensures that even if your phone is unlocked, your financial data is sealed.

## 🛠 Tech Stack (Product Lead's Note)

Built with the absolute latest Android development standards to ensure long-term stability and lightning-fast performance:

*   **UI:** 100% Jetpack Compose (Material 3).
*   **Architecture:** Clean Architecture with MVI/MVVM pattern.
*   **Database:** Room (Offline-first, encrypted-ready).
*   **DI:** Hilt (Dagger) for robust dependency management.
*   **Async:** Kotlin Coroutines & Flow for reactive data streams.
*   **Automation:** WorkManager for reliable background recurring syncs and reminders.
*   **Media:** Coil & Photo Picker API for seamless receipt handling.
